2. Description of Service

Ripe is a social coordination app for climbers. It enables users to log climbing session intent, coordinate with friends, check in at gyms, and create a private photo journal of shared sessions. Most social features are limited to mutual friends. Ripe also offers an optional, off-by-default feature called Discover Climbers: when you turn it on, a limited preview of your plan at a gym (your display name, photo, planned time, and climb type) becomes visible to other climbers who have also turned it on at that same gym, even if you are not friends. There are no open public feeds — discovery is symmetric and requires both people to opt in.
